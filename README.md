# Safe Opportunity Nigeria

A comprehensive practical guide to identifying fake jobs, recruitment scams, travel offers, and fraudulent international opportunities.

## Overview

This repository provides resources to help job seekers, travelers, and opportunity-seekers in Nigeria and globally identify and avoid scams. Whether you're looking for employment abroad, considering travel opportunities, or exploring international programs, use these resources to verify opportunities and protect yourself.

## Quick Start

1. **Before accepting any opportunity**, review the [15-Point Verification Checklist](./VERIFICATION_CHECKLIST.md)
2. **Check each point** carefully against the opportunity being considered
3. **Trust red flags** - if something feels wrong, investigate further
4. **Verify independently** using official channels and government resources
5. **Ask questions** - legitimate opportunities welcome scrutiny

## Common Scam Types

This guide helps identify scams in:
- ❌ Fake job postings and employment scams
- ❌ Recruitment agency fraud
- ❌ Travel and tourism scams
- ❌ Education program fraud
- ❌ Investment and business opportunity scams
- ❌ Visa sponsorship fraud
- ❌ Romance and relationship scams (work-related)

## Key Principles

✅ **Legitimate opportunities:**
- Never require upfront payment
- Come through verified channels
- Have transparent terms and conditions
- Welcome verification and questions
- Offer clear communication and processes
- Have traceable and established business history

⚠️ **Be cautious of:**
- Unsolicited job offers
- Pressure to decide quickly
- Requests for personal financial information
- Opportunities that sound too good to be true
- Communication through unofficial channels
- Unverifiable claims or promises

### Reporting Scams
If you encounter a suspected scam:
1. Document all communications and evidence
2. Report to local police and relevant authorities
3. Report to the platform or job board
4. Alert others in relevant communities
5. Contact government consumer protection agencies
