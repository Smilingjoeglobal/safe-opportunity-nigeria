Inside the guide

Practical checks before money changes hands

The book helps readers slow down, gather evidence and make informed decisions without treating any opportunity as automatically safe.

01

Check the organisation

Confirm identities, registration details, official contact channels and whether the organisation can be independently traced.

02

Examine the offer

Spot pressure tactics, inconsistent documents, suspicious payment instructions and claims that cannot be verified.

03

Protect your information

Handle passports, bank information and personal records carefully while you investigate the opportunity.
