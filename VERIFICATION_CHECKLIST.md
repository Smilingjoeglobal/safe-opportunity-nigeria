# Safe Opportunity Nigeria - 15 Point Verification Checklist

A practical guide to identifying fake jobs, recruitment scams, travel offers, and international opportunities.

## How to Use This Checklist

This 15-point verification checklist helps you identify fraudulent job offers, recruitment scams, and questionable international opportunities. Check each point carefully before committing to any opportunity.

---

## Verification Points

### 1. **Verify the Company Legitimacy**
- [ ] Research the company on official business registries
- [ ] Check company website for professional design and contact information
- [ ] Verify registration with regulatory bodies
- [ ] Look for physical office addresses (not just P.O. boxes)
- [ ] Contact the company through official channels to confirm job posting

### 2. **Check Job Posting Details**
- [ ] Ensure the job description is detailed and specific
- [ ] Verify job title and responsibilities are clearly defined
- [ ] Confirm salary range is provided and competitive
- [ ] Check that location and work arrangement (remote/in-office) are specified
- [ ] Look for red flags: vague descriptions, no location, unrealistic salaries

### 3. **Identify Recruitment Process Red Flags**
- [ ] Be cautious of job offers without interviews
- [ ] Avoid companies that hire without verifying qualifications
- [ ] Reject positions offering employment with minimal requirements
- [ ] Be suspicious of pressure to decide quickly
- [ ] Legitimate employers conduct thorough vetting processes

### 4. **Watch for Financial Requests**
- [ ] **NEVER** pay upfront fees to apply or secure a job
- [ ] Be wary of requests for:
  - Processing fees
  - Training fees
  - Deposit/collateral
  - Visa fees (legitimate employers handle this)
  - Registration fees
- [ ] Legitimate employers pay YOU, not the other way around

### 5. **Evaluate Communication Patterns**
- [ ] Verify email addresses match official company domains
- [ ] Check for spelling and grammar errors in communications
- [ ] Be suspicious of poor English or generic messaging
- [ ] Legitimate companies use professional communication
- [ ] Watch for inconsistencies in contact information

### 6. **Verify Recruiter Credentials**
- [ ] Confirm recruiter works for the stated company
- [ ] Look up recruiter on LinkedIn with company affiliation
- [ ] Verify recruiter's phone number through official company channels
- [ ] Be cautious of recruiters from unofficial email addresses
- [ ] Request official recruiter identification

### 7. **Check Contract and Employment Terms**
- [ ] Review any contract carefully before signing
- [ ] Look for unclear or ambiguous terms
- [ ] Be wary of contracts in foreign languages you don't understand
- [ ] Avoid one-sided agreements heavily favoring the employer
- [ ] Ensure contract includes clear terms of service, pay, and benefits

### 8. **Validate Payment Methods**
- [ ] Ensure payment is through legitimate banking channels
- [ ] Avoid wire transfers to unknown accounts
- [ ] Be suspicious of cryptocurrency payments
- [ ] Check bank details match official company information
- [ ] Legitimate employers use traceable payment methods

### 9. **Confirm References and Background**
- [ ] Ask the company for references from current employees
- [ ] Attempt to contact employees independently
- [ ] Research company reviews on legitimate job sites
- [ ] Check social media presence and employee testimonials
- [ ] Look for consistent company history and reputation

### 10. **Verify Visa and Travel Sponsorship Offers**
- [ ] Check that visa sponsorship follows legal procedures
- [ ] Be cautious of "guaranteed visa approval"
- [ ] Verify immigration requirements with official government sources
- [ ] Ensure travel arrangements are booked through reputable agencies
- [ ] Ask for documentation from immigration/labor authorities

### 11. **Evaluate Training and Development Programs**
- [ ] Be wary of programs promising unrealistic outcomes
- [ ] Verify accreditation of training providers
- [ ] Check that training requirements are relevant to the job
- [ ] Confirm training is provided FREE by employer
- [ ] Research trainer credentials and experience

### 12. **Review Social Media and Online Presence**
- [ ] Check company social media accounts for authenticity
- [ ] Look for recent activity and legitimate engagement
- [ ] Verify social media handles match official company information
- [ ] Be suspicious of new or inactive accounts
- [ ] Check for positive employee reviews and testimonials

### 13. **Assess Interview Process Quality**
- [ ] Expect multiple rounds of professional interviews
- [ ] Be suspicious of immediate job offers without proper vetting
- [ ] Verify interview panels include legitimate company staff
- [ ] Check that interviews are conducted through official channels
- [ ] Legitimate companies take time to find the right fit

### 14. **Verify International Opportunity Legitimacy**
- [ ] Research the country's labor laws and regulations
- [ ] Check visa requirements through official government sources
- [ ] Verify housing and living arrangements
- [ ] Ensure working conditions meet international standards
- [ ] Look for testimonials from others who worked the opportunity

### 15. **Trust Your Instincts**
- [ ] If something feels wrong, it probably is
- [ ] Don't ignore red flags to secure a job or opportunity
- [ ] Take time to verify information before committing
- [ ] Ask questions and request documentation
- [ ] Contact job verification services or local authorities if suspicious

---

## Common Scam Red Flags

🚩 **Immediate Warning Signs:**
- Upfront payment requests of any kind
- Pressure to decide quickly
- Too good to be true salary or benefits
- Unprofessional communication
- Vague job descriptions
- No formal interview process
- Requests for personal banking details
- Job offers via SMS or social media DMs

---

## Resources for Verification

### Official Government Sources
- **Nigeria Labour Ministry:** Labour regulations and job market information
- **Nigerian Immigration Service:** Visa and travel document verification
- **State Employment Services:** Local job listings and employer verification

### Online Verification Tools
- LinkedIn company verification
- Google Business verification
- Company registration databases
- Industry-specific professional bodies

### Reporting Scams
If you encounter a suspected scam:
1. Document all communications
2. Report to local authorities
3. Report to the job board/platform
4. Warn others (online forums, social media)
5. Contact relevant government agencies

---

## Important Reminders

✅ **Always Remember:**
- Legitimate opportunities rarely come with upfront costs
- Real employers contact YOU; you don't always chase them
- Take time to research and verify
- When in doubt, ask questions
- Your safety and financial security come first
- Trust established, verifiable sources

**Stay safe and make informed decisions!**
